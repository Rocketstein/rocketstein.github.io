Technical Pipeline

The tooling separates artist authoring from runtime evaluation: editor-authored profiles, flow maps, and presets are stored as reusable assets, then consumed by the storm actor to drive cloud-material parameters at runtime.

Engineering challenge

Hiding noise-wrap discontinuities

Long-running cloud motion eventually needs its scrolling noise offset to wrap. Resetting a single offset directly creates a visible pop in the volumetric density field, so I used a dual-phase sampling scheme to hide that reset.

Problem

A continuously increasing sampling offset cannot run forever. Wrapping it back into range causes the sampled 3D noise field to jump between unrelated positions.

Approach

Sample two staggered offsets and crossfade their decoded LF and HF noise results. One phase can wrap while the other remains stable, keeping the visible density continuous.

Trade-off

The transition costs an additional set of 3D noise samples, trading some shader work for seamless long-running motion without a visible reset.
