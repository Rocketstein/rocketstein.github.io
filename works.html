03Published Unreal plugin4-person team

Volumetric Super Storm

An art-directable real-time storm-authoring system extending Unreal Engine’s native Volumetric Cloud workflow.

My role

Vertical-profile authoring, profile sequencing, flow-map encoding, local wind motion, and shader behaviour for stable continuous scrolling.

  • Implemented artist-facing tools for freely designing a storm’s vertical density profile and reusing the result as an asset.
  • Connected profile transformations to a sequencing workflow for optional changes over time.
  • Encoded local wind direction and strength into RGBA flow maps and applied the data to three-dimensional noise motion.
  • Used dual-phase sampling and blending to avoid visible overscrolling and hard reset artefacts.

04Custom-engine gameTeam project

Engine Dive — Infinite Runner

A three-lane runner combining C++ engine systems with Lua gameplay and a complete title-to-results flow.

My role

Collision shapes and overlap flow, procedural map chunks, obstacle systems, the Imposter Gizmo mechanic, Player Camera Manager, and Camera Modifiers.

  • Implemented box, sphere, and capsule components with every supported shape pairing and begin/end overlap delivery.
  • Built runtime chunk generation and recycling, probabilistic obstacle placement, and constraints that preserve a traversable lane.
  • Created a fake editor gizmo that captures and transforms obstacles as a hostile gameplay mechanic.
  • Contributed final-POV calculation, camera modifiers, shake, fade, and C++–Lua gameplay integration.
